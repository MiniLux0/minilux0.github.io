Sobre Mí

I'm a physics student at Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería (UNI) in Lima, Perú, driven by curiosity about the computational underpinnings of physical systems.

My interests sit at the intersection of computational physics, quantum computing, and machine learning — exploring how algorithms can simulate, predict, and reveal the behavior of complex systems.

I work primarily in C++ for performance-critical simulations and use LaTeX for scientific documentation and academic reports.
